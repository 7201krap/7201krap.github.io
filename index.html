Ph.D. Student · Texas A&M University

Jin Hyun Park

Machine learning, computational neuroscience, and natural language processing.

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University, where I work as a graduate research and teaching assistant in the Brain Networks Lab under Dr. Yoonsuck Choe. My research focuses on (1) biologically inspired neural networks, (2) the evolution of predictive behavior, and (3) AI-driven smart floor-heating systems. I am currently a Physical AI Ph.D. Fellow at Doosan Corporation.

Before joining Texas A&M, I earned a B.Sc. in Computer Science with First Class Honors from the University of Bristol, and worked at Hyundai Motor Company and the Multimodal Language and Cognition Lab at Kyungpook National University. I have also interned as a machine learning engineer at LG Uplus, Samsung Life Insurance, and Tilda Corp.
