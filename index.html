About

I am a Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University, advised by Yoonsuck Choe in the Brain Networks Lab. My research sits where machine learning meets computational neuroscience, with a side interest in natural language processing. At Texas A&M, I have worked on neural networks that borrow structure from the visual cortex, on how prediction emerges through evolution, and, somewhat unexpectedly, on a learning-based thermostat for floor heating. Since February 2026 I have also been a Physical AI Ph.D. Fellow with Doosan Corporation.

I received a B.Sc. in Computer Science with First Class Honours from the University of Bristol in 2021, where I worked with Rui Ponte Costa on the representations learned under different learning rules. Before starting my Ph.D., I was a research assistant in the Multimodal Language and Cognition Lab at Kyungpook National University and an intern at Hyundai Motor Company. Summers have been spent as a machine learning engineer at LG Uplus, Samsung Life Insurance, and Tilda Corp., where the data was considerably less tidy than in any paper I had read.
