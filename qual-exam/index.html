Computer Science and Engineering Ph.D. Qualifier Exam

Notes for preparing for the qualifier exam in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Texas A&M University.

The Ph.D. qualifier exam in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at Texas A&M University is the ETS Major Field Test (MFT) in Computer Science. Details of the test are available from ETS: the test description (PDF) and the test content page. I strongly recommend starting with the Useful Materials page to get a sense of how the exam is structured.

My advice is to solve as many problems as possible rather than going through all of the undergraduate computer science material. The exam does not cover everything you saw as an undergraduate. Instead, there is a pool of questions that repeats from test to test. I learned this because I took the exam twice: new questions are written for each sitting, but a good number are duplicated from previous exams.

So, before the exam, work through as many past and sample questions as you can, and make sure you understand the ones you have solved. In my first attempt I went through all of the undergraduate material and did not pass. In my second attempt I focused on previous and sample questions and tried to understand every one of them, and I passed.
