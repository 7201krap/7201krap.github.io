Useful materials

Practice tests and references. Work through these first; they are the best predictor of what the real exam looks like.

The old GRE Computer Science subject test and the ETS Major Field Test are close relatives, so the GRE practice tests are very good preparation. Each link below is a PDF on Google Drive. Where a worked solution set exists, it is listed under the test.
